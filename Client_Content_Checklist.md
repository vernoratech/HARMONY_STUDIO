# Website Content Requirement Checklist

Please provide the following details to help us personalize your website. You can provide these in a Word document, a shared Folder (Google Drive/Dropbox), or directly via email.

---

## 1. About Us Page

This page tells your story and builds trust with your members.

### A Introduction & History

- **Company Name:** (e.g., Harmony Studio)
- **Founded Year:** (e.g., Since 2018)
- **Introductory Text:** A short 1-2 paragraph history of the studio, why it was started, and its background.
- **Main Visuals:**
  - **Image:** 1 High-quality landscape image of the studio or team.
  - **Video:** 1 Dance or session video (can be an Instagram link or a raw MP4 file).

### B Mission, Vision, & Values

- **Mission Statement:** What is your core purpose today? (e.g., "To inspire through movement...")
- **Vision Statement:** Where do you see the studio in 5 years? (e.g., "To be the global heartbeat of fitness...")
- **Core Values:** 3 to 5 keywords or short phrases that define your culture (e.g., Inclusivity, Excellence, Community).

### C Testimonials (Client Reviews)

- **Review Text:** 3 to 5 positive reviews from your students.
- **Student Details:** Name, Role (e.g., "Yoga Student"), and a small profile picture for each.

### D Why Choose Us?

- **Key Highlights:** 3 unique selling points (e.g., "150+ Classes Weekly", "Expert Mentors", "Premium Facilities").
- **Visuals:** 2-4 gallery-style images of different sessions (Yoga, Dance, etc.) to show variety.

---

## 2. Contact Us Page

Where your future members can reach you.

- **Mobile Number:** Primary contact number(s).
- **Email ID:** Professional email address for inquiries.
- **Address:** Full physical location with any specific landmark.
- **Social Media Links:** Links to your Instagram, Facebook, Twitter, and LinkedIn profiles.
- **Working Hours:**
  - **Weekdays:** (e.g., Mon to Sat: 8 AM - 9 PM)
  - **Weekends:** (e.g., Sunday: Closed or Specific Timing)

---

## 3. Services Page

A breakdown of what you offer. For each service (Dance, Yoga, Zumba, Aerobics), please provide:

- **Catchy Tagline:** (e.g., "Express Your Soul" for Dance).
- **Short Description:** A 2-3 sentence overview.
- **Detailed Introduction:** 1 paragraph explaining the philosophy of the class.
- **The Experience:** What is it like to be in the class? (Music, atmosphere, equipment).
- **Who It Is For:** List 3-4 types of people who would benefit (e.g., "Complete Beginners", "Desk Workers").
- **Key Features:** List of styles or specific sub-classes (e.g., "Hatha", "Vinyasa", "Power Yoga").

---

## 4. Gallery Page

A visual showcase of your studio's energy.

- **Category-wise Images:** Please provide 5-10 high-resolution images for each of these categories:
  - **Dance**
  - **Yoga**
  - **Zumba**
  - **Aerobics**
  - **General/Facilities** (Reception, Lounge, Yoga Halls).
- **Action Shots:** Dynamic photos of people in movement.
- **Facility Shots:** Clean photos of the empty studio spaces.

---

## 5. Team (Instructors)

If you want to feature your trainers:

- **Name & Specialty:** (e.g., "Priya Sharma - Head of Yoga").
- **Short Bio:** 1 paragraph about their experience and teaching style.
- **Experience Level:** Number of years teaching.
- **Profile Picture:** Professional headshot.
